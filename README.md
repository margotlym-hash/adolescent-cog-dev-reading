# Adolescent Cognitive Development — Reading Notes & Mini-Review

Independent reading and research training in adolescent developmental cognitive & social-affective neuroscience, with quantitative methods.

**Seed question**: How do adolescents and adults differ in processing language, emotion, and social information — and what neural mechanisms underlie these changes?

## Structure
- `notes/` — one-page notes per paper (starting with 5 review articles)
- `mini-review/` — a ~3,000-word synthesis (in progress)
- `data-demo/` — small analysis pipeline on public adolescent datasets (e.g., ABCD / Penn LEAD), coming in 2027

## About
Background: 4 years in data analytics, transitioning into developmental cognitive neuroscience. This repo documents the reading notes, the writing, and the process of learning to do research.
